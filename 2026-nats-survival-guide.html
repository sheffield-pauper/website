Unofficial 2026 Pauper Nationals Survival Guide

As a sheffield local, I'd thought I'd make this quick resource for people visiting sheffield just for the 2026 pauper nationals.
Pauper Events All events are being hosted at Patriot Games
  • Friday 27th: 3v3 Unified Teams - 17:30 start - Tickets and Info
  • Saturday 28th: Pauper nationals - 09:00 start
  • Sunday 29th: Casual Pauper Sundays - 12:00 start
Coffee recommendations I am not a coffee drinker, but I'm told by every coffee lover in sheffield that this is the best spot: Husk, a 9 minute walk from patriot games.

Kinfolk is slightly closer, but doesn't recieve the same fervor.
Food recommendations Here's a list of my favourite food spots within walking distance from Patriot Games that do takeaway options:
  • The Street Food Chef - Very tasty burrito's - 6 minute walk
  • YUTU Sushi & Korean - Good bibimbap and Katsu - 6 minute walk
  • Unit Burgers - Delicious fried chicken burgers - 12 minute walk
  • Yum Yum Bites - Personal favourite, annoyingly only takes cash - 6 minute walk
And a couple that are for if you go out like a buster and need somewhere to sit and cry:
  • Birdhouse - Pancakes, likely to be very busy/full - 4 minute walk
  • Kollective - Good coffee, cake and brunch/lunch options but always a long wait - 7 minute walk
  • Hideaway - Great food, but also good cocktails.
And post tournament, a good spot for a big group is Cambridge Street Collective, a food hall with a range of quality (majoritively good) - 14 minute walk.
Pub/Drinks recommendations These are all within some kind of walking distance from Patriot games.
  • The Rutland Arms - The local pub for pauper players, very small
  • Sheffield Tap - Beloved by local mono red experts
  • Hideaway - Good cocktails, but also great food
  • The Old Shoe - ~20 minute walk
  • The Red Deer - ~20 minute walk
